# Photo brief

The site currently uses the old cartoon portrait as an explicitly labelled artifact. A later pass needs real, current photographs—not stock images and not a staged corporate portrait session.

Please provide original, high-resolution files for:

1. **Current relaxed portrait** — Eugene looking like himself, in natural light, with room around the face for responsive cropping.
2. **Teaching or explaining** — Eugene at a board, table, or workbench. Avoid identifiable students unless written permission is documented; an over-the-shoulder or hands-only composition is fine.
3. **Hardware or workbench** — a robot, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, sensors, wiring, tools, or a half-finished mechanism in its real environment.
4. **Plant or agricultural automation** — the tomato, camera, pump, sensor, growing setup, or a current equivalent. The apparatus matters more than perfection.
5. **One unmistakably personal image** — something friends would recognize as Eugene. The boat is acceptable.

## Practical notes

- Landscape and portrait orientations are both useful; take a few of each.
- Prefer honest ambient light and visible context over a blank corporate background.
- Keep original files and metadata; do not send screenshots from a messaging app.
- Avoid logos, screens with private information, student names, house addresses, and readable credentials.
- Record the photographer and confirm permission for public website use.
